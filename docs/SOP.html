SEC. 1

Purpose & scope

This SOP describes how plant IT/MES support staff use the Call Log & Support Management System (CLMS) to record, route, and resolve support calls raised across Chakan Plant and Nashik Plant, and how to operate its reporting, master-data, and administration functions.

It applies to every person who logs into CLMS: shop-floor supervisors reporting problems, support engineers attending them, support managers routing and overseeing work, and administrators maintaining master data and user accounts.

SEC. 3

Getting started

3.1 — Logging in

  1. Open CLMS in a browser and go to the Login page.

  2. Enter your Employee ID (not an email address — e.g. EMP001) and your password.

  3. Click Login. You'll land on the Dashboard.

3.2 — Forgotten password

CLMS has no outbound email service, so self-service reset-by-email isn't available. Contact your Administrator and ask them to reset it from Administration › Users › Reset Password.

3.3 — Finding your way around

The left sidebar is grouped into four sections: Call Management (create/search/list calls), Reports, Master Configuration, and Administration. Which groups you see depends on your role (Section 2).

SEC. 4

The call lifecycle

Every call moves through a fixed sequence of statuses. CLMS enforces this on the server — you cannot close a call that hasn't been resolved, or resolve one that hasn't been assigned, regardless of role.

Open→ Assigned→ In Progress→ Resolved→ Closed
On Hold can be entered from Assigned or In Progress, and returns to In Progress via Start Progress.
Reopened is the only way back in from Closed — requires a written reason, then re-enters at In Progress via Start Progress.
A call can be handed over to a different engineer at any point before it is Closed or Cancelled — this does not change its status.
StatusMeaningWho can move it forward
OpenJust created, not yet assigned to an engineer.Admin / Support Manager (Assign)
AssignedAn engineer is on the hook but hasn't started work.Admin / Support Manager / attending Support Engineer
In ProgressActively being worked.same as above — can Resolve or put On Hold
On HoldPaused (e.g. waiting on a part or vendor).same as above — Start Progress to resume
ResolvedFix applied, root cause recorded (ICA/PCA).Admin / Support Manager / Supervisor (Close)
ClosedDone. Closing time and total time taken are locked in.Admin / Support Manager / Supervisor (Reopen)
ReopenedIssue recurred after closure.Admin / Support Manager / attending Support Engineer
SEC. 5

Creating a call

AdminSupport ManagerSupport EngineerSupervisorViewer

  1. Go to Call Management › Create Call.

  2. Fill in Location, then Shop (the shop list loads once a location is picked).

  3. Choose Call Type (User Support / Shopfloor Concern), Module, and optionally Application Type.

  4. Choose Problem Category and, if relevant, the specific Problem — this list narrows based on the category and module chosen.

  5. Pick who reported it (Problem Reported By, from the Employee master), and write the Description & Cause of Problem.

  6. Set Call Category and Priority.

  7. If this is a line-loss incident, switch Is Line Loss to Yes — see Section 10 for the fields that unlocks.

  8. A Support Engineer's Reported Date & Time and Attended By are locked to "now" and "yourself." Admin/Support Manager can back-date and assign to anyone.

  9. Attach files if needed (PNG, JPG, JPEG, PDF, XLSX, TXT, LOG — max 10 MB each), then click Save Call, or Save & Continue to immediately start another.

NoteThe call number (e.g. CALL-2026-000007) is generated automatically and sequentially — it cannot be set or edited.
SEC. 6

Assigning & handing over

6.1 — Assign an engineer

AdminSupport Manager

  1. Open the call's Details page and click Assign.

  2. Pick the engineer, add an optional remark, and confirm. A call still Open moves to Assigned automatically.

6.2 — Hand over to another engineer

AdminSupport ManagerSupport Engineer (own call)

  1. From Details, click Handover.

  2. Choose the receiving engineer and write a Reason (required). The call's status does not change — only who's attending it.

NoteBoth actions notify the receiving engineer via the bell icon (Section 12).
SEC. 7

Progress, resolve & close

AdminSupport ManagerSupport Engineer (own call)+ Supervisor for Close

7.1 — Standard path

  1. On Details, click Start Progress to move Assigned/On Hold/Reopened into In Progress.

  2. Click Resolve, fill in ICA / PCA (root cause & corrective action — required) and any remarks, and confirm.

  3. An Admin, Support Manager, or Supervisor then clicks Close on the Resolved call. Closing time and total time taken are recorded automatically and cannot be edited afterward.

7.2 — Resolve & Close (fixed on the spot)

Many calls are reported and fixed in the same visit. Rather than clicking Assign, then Resolve, then Close separately, Admin/Support Manager can use one shortcut:

  1. On Details, click Resolve & Close (appears on any call that isn't already Resolved/Closed/Cancelled).

  2. Enter ICA / PCA and confirm. CLMS assigns the engineer already named at creation (or restarts progress if the call was Reopened), resolves it, and closes it — one submit, three logged steps, full history preserved.

NoteThis shortcut needs both Assign's and Close's authority, so it's Admin/Support Manager only — narrower than either action alone.
SEC. 8

Reopening a call

AdminSupport ManagerSupervisor

  1. On a Closed call's Details page, click Reopen.

  2. Write a Reason (required) and confirm. The call returns to Reopened; its Closing Date and Time Taken are cleared until it's resolved again.

The attending engineer is notified (Section 12) so they know to pick it back up.

SEC. 9

Editing a previous entry

Use Call Management › Edit Previous Entry to search for and correct a call already logged — a broader search than "All Calls," with per-field edit tiers:

Field tierExamplesWho can edit
System-controlledCall Number, Created By, Status, Time TakenNobody — always read-only
Special permissionLocation, Shop, Module, Reported By/Date, Attended By, Line Loss fieldsAdmin / Support Manager only
NormalDescription, Problem Category/Problem, Call Category, Priority, ICA/PCA, RemarksAdmin / Support Manager / the attending Support Engineer
CautionA Closed call is entirely read-only to a Support Engineer — only Admin/Support Manager can still edit it. Every saved change is written to the call's activity timeline with old & new values.
SEC. 10

Line loss & downtime

When Is Line Loss is set to Yes on Create or Edit, four fields become required:

FieldCaptures
Line / AreaWhich line or area stopped producing
Downtime StartWhen the line actually stopped
Downtime EndWhen it resumed (optional while ongoing — duration then shows as "Ongoing")
Affected VehiclesHow many vehicles/units were lost to the stoppage, as a number (e.g. 4)

Duration is computed automatically from Start/End and cannot be typed in directly. All four values roll up into the Line Loss Report.

SEC. 11

Reports & exports

AdminSupport ManagerSupport EngineerSupervisorViewer

11.1 — Call Report

Group totals by Day, Month, Location, Shop, Module, Problem Category, or Status, filtered by date range, location, status and priority. Shows Total / Open / Closed / Line Loss counts and average resolution time per group, with a Grand Total row.

Export to Excel and Export to PDF download the exact filtered/grouped view as a spreadsheet or a landscape PDF; Print uses the browser's print dialog directly.

11.2 — Engineer Performance

Per engineer: total calls, open/in-progress/resolved/closed/reopened counts, average resolution time, critical-priority count, line-loss count, and SLA breaches — filterable by date range and location.

11.3 — SLA Report

Compares each call's actual resolution time against the target in SLA Configuration for its priority, filterable by priority, location, date range, and a "breached only" toggle.

11.4 — Line Loss Report

Total incidents, total downtime, and total affected vehicles, broken down by shop and listed incident-by-incident with a link back to each call.

SEC. 13

Notifications

The bell icon in the top bar checks for updates every 30 seconds and shows an unread count. You're notified when:

EventWho's notified
A call is assigned to youThe engineer newly assigned
A call is handed over to youThe receiving engineer
A call is reopenedWhoever is currently attending it

Click a notification to mark it read and jump straight to the call, or open View all for the full history and a Mark all as read action.

SEC. 15

User & role administration

Admin only

From Administration › Users: create a user (Employee ID, name, designation, location, role, initial password), edit their details or role, deactivate/reactivate, or force-reset a password. You cannot deactivate your own account.

Administration › Roles is read-only — it shows user counts per role and the permission matrix from Section 2. Roles are fixed by design and can't be created, edited, or deleted here.

SEC. 16

Audit log

Admin only

Every login, logout, failed login, master-data change, user/role change, and call status change is recorded with who, when, and old/new values. Filter by action type or search by user, entity, or value.

SEC. 17

Troubleshooting

"I can't close a call"

Close only lights up once a call is Resolved. If it's still Open or Assigned, either Resolve it first, or use Resolve & Close (Section 7.2) if Admin/Support Manager.

"Save Call fails and I land on a blank page"

This usually means the browser tried to follow an HTTPS redirect to a certificate it doesn't trust. Ask your administrator to run:

dotnet dev-certs https --trust

and accept the Windows trust prompt that appears.

"A status/priority badge shows as invisible or white text"

Hard-refresh the page (Ctrl+F5). This was a one-time issue tied to a Bootstrap version upgrade and is resolved as of this version.

"Attended By" won't save / Create Call rejects submission

Check that every field marked required in Section 5 is filled — in particular, Admin/Support Manager must explicitly pick an engineer in Attended By rather than leaving the default blank selection.
